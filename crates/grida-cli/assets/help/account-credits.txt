Usage: grida account credits [--org <slug> | --org-id <id>] [--json] [--no-input]

Read cached USD credits and billing eligibility for an organization.
Omit the selector only when you belong to exactly one organization.
Numeric slugs remain slugs; --org-id selects a positive numeric ID.
An unknown balance is distinct from zero. Cached eligibility is not a
guarantee that generation will succeed.

--no-input suppresses terminal interaction; the OS keyring may still request access.
Documentation: https://grida.co/docs/cli/account
