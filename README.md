# Grida

**An open-source canvas for design and creative work.**

Design graphics, create images, and make presentations with AI in Grida Desktop.

[Download for desktop](https://grida.co/downloads) · [Website](https://grida.co) · [Documentation](https://grida.co/docs)

Available for macOS, Windows, and Linux.

## Make room for your ideas

Bring images, type, and vectors together on an open canvas. Start with your own work, import from Figma, or find a reference in the [Library](https://grida.co/library). Use AI to explore a direction and create assets, then keep shaping the result with your design tools.

Keep your projects in your own folders. Bring your own provider keys for AI, or use supported local text models with Ollama.

## Beyond the canvas

- [Grida CLI](https://grida.co/docs/cli) — Generate media from your terminal, scripts, or your own agent. Desktop doesn't need to be running.
- [Grida FX](https://github.com/gridaco/fx) — Turn models, tools, and checks into reusable asset workflows. A standalone project, currently in preview.
- [Grida AI Gateway](https://grida.co/ai/gateway) — One API for creative models. The standalone service is coming soon.

You can also explore [SVG](https://grida.co/svg), [Forms](https://grida.co/forms), and [Database](https://grida.co/database).

## Open source

Grida is built on the [nothing graphics engine](https://github.com/gridaco/nothing). Explore the [repository guide](./REPOSITORY.md), [contribute](./CONTRIBUTING.md), or join the [community](https://grida.co/join-slack).
