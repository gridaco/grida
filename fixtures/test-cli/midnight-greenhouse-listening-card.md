# Midnight greenhouse listening card

## Prompt

> Use Grida CLI to make a thirty-second listening card for a fictional midnight
> greenhouse. First illustrate a small glass conservatory in a rainy city garden:
> terracotta pots, broad tropical leaves and a brass watering can by the door.
> Make a second image using the first as a reference: the same building and
> objects at night, with warm amber lights and deep blue rain outside. Preserve
> the composition so the two images feel like a change of time, not location.
>
> Write and generate a short, calm narration: "After the city goes quiet, the
> greenhouse keeps its own time. Rain taps the glass. Leaves hold the warmth.
> Stay a moment. There is nothing here that needs to hurry." Use an ordinary
> available voice, without imitating a named person. From the finished images,
> write a music direction and generate one instrumental bed with sparse felt
> piano and soft organic percussion. The music must leave room for the voice.
>
> Make a local HTML listening card with both images and a thirty-second audio mix,
> plus the separate narration and music stems. Include the original requests,
> receipts and a short report on image continuity, pronunciation and the mix.
> Propose the exact calls before running. If music access is unavailable, report
> that limitation before spending; do not relabel a sound effect as music.

## Handoffs

The first image conditions the second. The external harness describes the final
artwork in a text music prompt; no image-to-music model input is implied. Narration
and music are mixed locally. Current music execution requires GG and an eligible
organization; the personal BYOK keys alone do not satisfy this brief. Speech also
requires an explicit available voice. Confirm the music operation's duration
contract before choosing or trimming the final mix.

## Judge the result

- The conservatory, pots and watering can retain their identity and placement.
- Narration contains the intended words and remains intelligible over the music.
- The music is instrumental, supports the artwork's mood, and is separately
  available from the final mix.
- The card works locally; the report distinguishes image conditioning, harness
  interpretation, generated media and local audio editing.
