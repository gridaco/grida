# Paper boat film

## Prompt

> Make an eight-second miniature film with Grida CLI: a tiny folded yellow paper
> boat travels across a rain puddle outside a closed flower shop at blue hour.
> Violet window reflections, warm light behind the door, a low camera close to
> the water, and one small leaf caught on the bow. No people, logos or lettering.
>
> Generate the opening image first, then give that actual image to a video model.
> Preserve the boat's folds, the leaf and the shop geometry while the boat drifts
> forward and the camera gently follows. I want generated motion, not a zoom on
> the still. Make a separate eight-second soundtrack of light rain, water lapping
> and a faint distant shop-door bell. No narration or music.
>
> Assemble a local MP4 using the generated video and the separate soundtrack,
> replacing any original video audio. Keep the original image, video, audio,
> request files and receipts. Give me a contact sheet and a short report on visual
> continuity, sound timing and every step the CLI could not perform itself.
> Limit the first attempt to one image, one video and one sound effect. Before
> running, explain how the image reaches the video model and get my approval for
> the calls and any hosting needed. If that handoff is unavailable, stop there
> and document it; don't substitute an unrelated text-to-video result.

## Handoffs

Image bytes → authorized HTTPS image URL → image-to-video; video and independently
generated SFX → local muxing. The current CLI requires HTTPS for video image input
and provides no upload command. A local path, data URL or private loopback server
is not an accepted substitute. This brief cannot complete under a strictly local
artifact-handoff constraint today.

## Judge the result

- The opening image is demonstrably the video input, and the boat/leaf remain
  recognizable across sampled frames.
- Motion is generated by the video operation; camera movement does not conceal
  a substituted slideshow or unrelated scene.
- The final MP4 has the intended duration and a single intended audio mix, with
  no accidental double soundtrack or harsh truncation.
- Any upload destination, retention, local editing and blocked handoff are
  disclosed. Provider result URLs are not copied into a shareable report.
