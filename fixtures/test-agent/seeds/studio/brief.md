# Client brief: Hearth & Glaze spring launch

Hearth & Glaze is a two-person ceramics studio in a converted bakery. They sell
small-batch stoneware online and at weekend markets, and they are launching a
six-piece spring collection called **Thaw**.

## Audience

People who buy one good mug instead of six cheap ones. Mostly 25–45, they find
the studio on Instagram and through the newsletter.

## Personality

Warm, unhurried, a little wry. Hand-made, not rustic-cute. Think glazed clay,
morning light, flour on a counter. Avoid anything that looks like a supermarket
promotion: no starbursts, no "SALE", no exclamation marks.

## Visual direction

- The current logo is just the name typed in a serif. They want a real mark: a
  simple shape that still reads at 48 px (the Instagram avatar).
- Colours should come from the glazes: oatmeal, celadon, iron red, ink.
- Generous margins. Let the product names breathe.
- They have no product photography yet. Use flat illustration or pure type and
  colour; do not use stock imagery.

## Launch

- The collection goes live on **Saturday 14 March**, 9:00.
- Newsletter subscribers get first access on Friday 13 March.

## Constraints

- No web fonts or external assets: the studio's site builder strips them. Use
  system font stacks.
- Everything must be accessible: text contrast at WCAG AA or better.
