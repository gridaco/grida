# Plans and billing

Grida offers a free plan, one simple monthly subscription, and Custom terms for
organizations with specific requirements.

## Plans

| Plan   |             Price | How to get it                                      |
| ------ | ----------------: | -------------------------------------------------- |
| Free   |                $0 | Start without a paid subscription.                 |
| Pro    | $20 USD per month | Upgrade from your organization's billing settings. |
| Custom |           Bespoke | [Contact Grida](https://grida.co/contact).         |

Pro is billed monthly per organization, not per member. Inviting or removing a
member does not change the base subscription price.

Custom is sales-led. Its price, billing schedule, support, deployment, and other
terms are defined in an agreement rather than a self-service checkout.

Team and annual subscriptions are not available for new purchase.

## AI credit

Free and Pro do not include a recurring AI-credit allowance. AI credit is
purchased separately and shared by the organization.

You can buy between $10 and $500 of credit in one checkout. The checkout shows
the credit you receive and the additional processing charge before you pay.
Automatic reload is available on Pro. Existing legacy Team subscriptions keep
access until they move to Pro. Custom agreements may define different AI-credit
terms.

## Manage billing

Organization owners can use billing settings to:

- view the current plan and, for Pro, its renewal date;
- review invoices;
- update the payment method;
- buy AI credit; and
- cancel a Pro subscription.

Pro renews each month until canceled. Cancellation takes effect at the end of
the current paid period, so the organization keeps Pro until that date.

## Refunds and billing errors

Subscription charges are not automatically prorated when a subscription is
canceled. Duplicate charges, billing errors, and unused accidental AI-credit
purchases are handled under the [refund policy](./refund-policy.md).

## Custom plans

Custom plans are for organizations that need tailored commercial terms,
deployment, integrations, or support. [Contact Grida](https://grida.co/contact)
to discuss the requirements.
